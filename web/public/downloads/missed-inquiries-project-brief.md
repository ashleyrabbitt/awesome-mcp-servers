# Recover missed inquiries

## Business goal

A small service business receives inquiries after closing. The office needs an owned callback request by the next shift.

## Inputs to supply

List staffed hours, service boundary, intake channels, queue owner, backup and callback target. Attach approved replies and three fictional inquiries.

## Requested delivery

Capture name, callback preference and request. Route each inquiry once. Confirm a booking only after the calendar returns a confirmation.

## Acceptance test

Replay one routine inquiry, one correction, one out-of-area request and one unavailable owner. Compare the delivered record with the caller’s final words.

## How we will measure it

Count valid handoffs, wrong promises, duplicate tickets and unanswered requests. A fabricated booking blocks rollout.

## Handoff

Give the office an export, queue owner instructions, fallback path, rollback steps and a retest date.

## Provider response

State your fixed pilot fee, recurring fees, timeline, dependencies, data access required and what changes would require a new quote.
