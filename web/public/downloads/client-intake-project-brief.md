# Turn an inquiry into a reviewed intake brief

## Business goal

A services team spends its first call reconstructing information a prospective client already supplied.

## Inputs to supply

Provide an approved intake form, required fields, CRM destination, retention instructions, review owner and three fictional submissions.

## Requested delivery

Draft one brief with requested outcome, budget if supplied, missing information, source references and assigned reviewer. Let the reviewer approve the CRM write.

## Acceptance test

Try a complete submission, a missing budget and a corrected contact preference. Verify the missing budget stays unknown and a retry creates one record.

## How we will measure it

Record correct fields out of expected fields, invented fields, duplicate records and minutes to approval against the current manual process.

## Handoff

Deliver the field mapping, permission list, sample export, exception queue, owner and training notes.

## Provider response

State your fixed pilot fee, recurring fees, timeline, dependencies, data access required and what changes would require a new quote.
