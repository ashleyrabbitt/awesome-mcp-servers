# Turn one owned webinar into three usable clips

## Business goal

A marketing team owns a webinar recording and wants clips that preserve the speaker’s meaning.

## Inputs to supply

Supply the owned recording, transcript, permission scope, brand terms, intended audience and approved CTA. Specify aspect ratio and caption style.

## Requested delivery

Deliver three captioned drafts, timecodes linking each quote to the original, editable captions, thumbnails and one review round. Obtain approval before publishing.

## Acceptance test

Check each opening in context, every name and number, safe caption margins and whether the CTA matches the source. Reject any edit that reverses a qualification.

## How we will measure it

Track accepted clips out of three, caption corrections, editor minutes to approval and cost per accepted clip.

## Handoff

Hand over exports, editable files, timestamps, permission record, retention date and the owner of final publication.

## Provider response

State your fixed pilot fee, recurring fees, timeline, dependencies, data access required and what changes would require a new quote.
