The series is linked from the existing archive. RSS and sitemap entries appear as articles release. FAQs are written for readers; no FAQ rich-result eligibility is claimed.

Google states that normal SEO principles apply to AI search features; there is no special AI markup required or inclusion guarantee. Clear answers, primary sources, useful examples and crawlable pages support discoverability, but do not guarantee rankings, citations, traffic or revenue.
